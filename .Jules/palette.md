## 2025-05-14 - [Nested Interactive Elements]
**Learning:** Nesting a `<button>` inside an `<a>` tag is invalid HTML and creates confusing experiences for screen reader users, who may hear two interactive elements for a single action.
**Action:** Use a `<span>` or `<div>` styled to look like a button inside the link to maintain visual design while ensuring semantic correctness and accessibility.
